## Problem 1

Evaluate the indefinite integral.

$$
\int 3e^{4x+7}\,dx
$$

Use $C$ for the constant of integration.

---
## Problem 2

Consider

$$
\int_1^3(12x-1)e^{6x^2-x}\,dx.
$$

Let

$$
u=6x^2-x.
$$

Rewrite the integrand and differential in terms of $u$.

$$
(12x-1)e^{6x^2-x}\,dx=\qquad du
$$
