Module 4 — Learning Materials and Practice Activities 🕓 3 - 5 hours

Intro

Understanding how to appropriately apply the Net Change Theorem and to build intuition for efficiently using substitution comes by practicing a variety of problem types. Below you find a broad range of reading and practice problems to support the mastery of this material.  Then we complete our study of basic antiderivatives by studying integrals of exponentials and logarithms.  As substitution will be used in almost every section going forward, it is recommended that you take advantage of as much practice as possible with the material given.
