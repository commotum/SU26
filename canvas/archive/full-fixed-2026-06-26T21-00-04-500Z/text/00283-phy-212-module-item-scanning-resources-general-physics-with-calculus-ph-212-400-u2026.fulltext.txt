    Instructions
To get a high-quality scan for uploading files, use a scanning app to scan your handwritten work, and upload your scanned document. Here are some resources to help you get started:

Get a clear scan that is automatically cropped and formatted into multi-page PDF files by installing a scanning app on your phone. Here are some recommended free apps:

Genius Scan  (Links to an external site.) (available for iPhone, iPad, and Android devices). Find it at the  Apple Store  (Links to an external site.) or  Google Play.  (Links to an external site.)
Microsoft Office Lens  (Links to an external site.) (available for iPhone, iPad and Android). Find the app at the Apple Store or Google Play.
CamScanner  (Links to an external site.) (available for iPhone and Android). This app has the most features, and the basic package is free. However, there are subscription options available if you want to pay for the more advanced features. Find this app at the Apple Store or Google Play.
Scanner Pro  (Links to an external site.) (available for iPhone and iPad only). 


If you do not have a smartphone for scanning your work, please scan your work from a home scanner, take screenshots of your work and submit those, or type out and attach a file of a written description of the steps you took.
