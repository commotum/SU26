Purpose
Experimental Goal: To estimate a conservative safety-line distance a typical beachgoer can realistically reach before a surge wave arrives.
In this lab, students will measure walking speed, estimate wave period from video, and model reaction time as a fraction of the wave cycle. They will propagate uncertainty through these quantities to estimate how much time a person has to move before a surge arrives and how far they could realistically travel. Using uncertainty intervals rather than single values, students will propose a conservative beach safety line and reflect on how uncertainty and human variability affect confidence in safety decisions.
