Introduction
We continue with definite and indefinite integrals by introducing the idea of substitution, with various strategies, and we begin looking at antiderivatives involving transcendental exponential and logarithmic functions.  With the tools we have developed, we will often need to employ both substitution and an elementary antiderivative to evaluate integrals going forward.
Module Learning Outcomes
After successful completion of this module, you will be able to: 

Apply the net-change theorem.
Find displacement, net area, and or a position function over a given interval.
Use substitution methods to evaluate definite/indefinite integrals.
Evaluate antiderivatives for exponentials and logarithms.
Discriminate between various integration methods.
