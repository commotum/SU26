Purpose
This discussion is for your group to coordinate and track progress on your lab report. Use it to share updates, confirm responsibilities, and ensure everyone is contributing on schedule. Your posts should reflect what you’ve completed, what’s in progress, and what still needs to be done before your group report due Saturday.
 
Discussion Instructions

To complete your individual discussion post, open the lab handout, then post a picture of your home setup AND post your answers to Questions Q1–Q5 in your group's discussion thread. (Due Wednesday) 

Your discussion is meant to initiate the lab exercise and begin the conversation that leads to your group lab report.

Coordinating With Your Group
Begin communicating with your lab group to prepare your group lab report, due Saturday. Use this discussion board, Zoom, or another platform to collaborate.

Start by if a new member has been added to your group this week, please introduce yourself and bring them up to speed.

You can identify your group members by clicking on “People” in the discussion. To contact them, you can:

Email them using your Outlook student account by searching their name in the directory,
Message them through the Canvas Inbox tool, or
Post an announcement within the discussion board.






The current Group Leader should:

Create a shared Google Doc for writing the group lab report.
Create a shared spreadsheet for sharing and recording measurements/calculations.
Share both files with all group members and ensure they have editing access.
Paste the link to the shared spreadsheet and Google Doc in this discussion thread by Thursday morning.



If no spreadsheet has been shared by then, any member is welcome to step in and create one. Communication and collaboration are everyone's responsibility! If you have unresponsive group members who have not contributed to the group report, please email to your Lab TA. Remember, all members are expected to contribute to both planning and writing, and the Group Leader’s role is to facilitate—not do all the work.
