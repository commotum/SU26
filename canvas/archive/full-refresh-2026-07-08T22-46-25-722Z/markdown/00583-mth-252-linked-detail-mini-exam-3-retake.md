Due Sep 2 by 11:59pm Points 50 Submitting an external tool Available after Mar 13 at 12am

Instructions
The Mini-Exam is accessible from Monday 12:00 am to Wednesday 11:59 pm (Pacific Time).
Notes: You may ONLY use a non-graphing calculator. NO additional resources, tools, webpages, homework, nor or peers may be used.
Download/Upload/View this exam from the assignment in Gradescope:  252-400-S23 course.
Once you Open/View the exam, you have 75 minutes to complete AND upload your exam solutions.  A timer is on the Gradescope page, top-right, and will continue even if you close the page.
This is a 50 minute exam, with an extra 25 minutes to scan/picture and submit, totally 75 minutes.  
Formatting
Print it (electronic pen or tablet is OK) completing it neatly showing all work right on the exam, and placing answers in the given boxes.
Can't Print?  You are invited to work on the exam on your own paper mirroring everything on the exam. Please do NOT rewrite all the problems. Just submit written solutions using this template.

Your final document should be One PDF file with 6 pages uploaded.

Grading

This activity is worth 50 points.

Practice Problems
Students need to have memorized antiderivatives for function types equivalent to:
Polynomials.
Poly-powers, sums/differences of power functions. 
Sine, cosine, and secant squared.
e^x and 1/x.
and substitution (U-Sub) applied to all of the function types above. 

Practice Problems and Info:

3.1: Integration by Parts - Students will only have to apply IBP once, not twice or more. Must have the IBP formula memorized.
Practice: 9, 11, 12, 16, 17, 41, 42.
3.2: Trig Integrals - Only the Pythagorean identity involving sine/cosine and tangent/secant will be on the mini-exam. NO 1/2-angle power-reduction identities on this mini-exam. 
Practice: 83, 84, 85, 109.
3.3: Trig Substitution - Only the structure/substitution for x = a sin(theta)  and  x =a tan(theta) will be on the mini-exam.
Be prepared for Setup-ONLY problems OR if given an antiderivative in theta, use a reference triangle to convert to the variable x and get a final answer. 
Practice trig sub problems all the way through and using the triangle to convert back to x. 
Identify (1) when you have converted and simplified the integral in theta, and (2) found an antiderivative and converted the antiderivative back to a function in "x"
Review OpenStax Example 3.21 to see an example of what was discussed at the end of class about having a theta as an antiderivative. 
Practice: 134, 137, 140, 153, 158.
3.4: Partial Fraction Decomposition - This will be a Setup-ONLY problem and possibly the need to appropriately equate coefficients. You will NOT have to solve for coefficients or evaluate integrals on this. 
Practice: 189, 188, 190, 191, 195, 202, 211
