Intro

The techniques we are finishing this week involve subtle details. The approach is determined by the structure or components of the integrand. Because of this, continue practice will strengthen our mathematical intuition.  This makes using all these techniques get easier with practice.  Below are a variety of examples, readings, and practice problems to support us with this effort.
