Instructions
The Mini-Exam is accessible from Monday 12:00 am to Wednesday 11:59 pm (Pacific Time).
Notes: You may ONLY use a non-graphing calculator. NO additional resources, tools, webpages, homework, nor or peers may be used.
Download/Upload/View this exam from the assignment in Gradescope:  252-400-S23 course.
Once you Open/View the exam, you have 75 minutes to complete AND upload your exam solutions.  A timer is on the Gradescope page, top-right, and will continue even if you close the page.
This is a 50 minute exam, with an extra 25 minutes to scan/picture and submit, totally 75 minutes.  
Formatting
Print it (electronic pen or tablet is OK) completing it neatly showing all work right on the exam, and placing answers in the given boxes.
Can't Print?  You are invited to work on the exam on your own paper mirroring everything on the exam. Please do NOT rewrite all the problems. Just submit written solutions using this template.

Your final document should be One PDF file with 6 pages uploaded.

Grading

This activity is worth 50 points.

Practice Problems
Students need to have memorized antiderivatives for function types equivalent to:
Polynomials.
Poly-powers, sums/differences of power functions. 
Sine, cosine, and secant squared.
e^x and 1/x.
and substitution (U-Sub) applied to all of the function types above. 

Practice Problems:

All graphs/images will be given on the Mini-Exam. 
For all topics below, be prepared to (1) setup the integral ONLY and NOT evaluate, or (2) setup the integral and evaluate.
2.1 - Area Between Curves: 3, 9, 10, 15, 21, 27
Be sure to practice integrating along the x-axis and separately the y-axis.
Be sure to practice solving for x or y based on how you are integrating. 
For a given region, compare/contrast how many integrals it will take if you integrate along the x-axis versus integrating along the y-axis.
Needing to complete the square will NOT be on this mini-exam. 
2.2 - Slicing, Disc/Washer Methods: 71, 72, 73, 77, 85, 93, 99
Be sure to practice cross-sectional slicing method with slices perpendicular to the x-axis and separately the y-axis. 
Be sure to practice drawing sample slices to identify "outer" radius (disk method) and "inner" radius (washer method). 
Be sure to practice revolving around axes parallel to the x-axis and separately ones parallel to the y-axis.
2.3 - Shell Method: 124, 133, 134, 141, 142
Be sure to practice drawing sample slices to identify "outer" and "inner" radii as well as "upper" and "lower" heights. 
Be sure to practice revolving around axes parallel to the y-axis and separately ones parallel to the x-axis.
2.2/2.3 Comparative analysis - Given a region and an axis of rotation, which method (disk/washer or shell or both) can be used to compute the volume using only one integral. 
2.4 - Arc Length: 171, 173, 174, 183, 186
Arc length formula will be given on exam, along with a relatively easy function. Students will have to correctly use the formula and evaluate the integral. Practice problems above reflect the type of functions to expect. Be prepared to evaluate the arc length formula. 
2.4 Surface Area is NOT covered in this course.
