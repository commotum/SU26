Unit 1 Discussion Board - Submitted in Week 1 (Required)

Purpose

Welcome to the class! It is helpful to get to know your classmates that you will be working with over the next 10 weeks. The purpose of this discussion board is to introduce yourself and engage with your classmates. 

Topic

Main Post (In approximately 200 words): 

Your main post will consist of two parts. In Part I: please introduce yourself. Please share only information that you are comfortable with sharing. Below are just some ideas to help you get started. 

What is your preferred name?
Where are you taking this class from?
What is your major? How are you liking your major so far?
What do you hope to gain from this course? Do you have any reservations about this course going in? Is there anything you look forward to about this course?
What are your interests/background/hobbies that you would like to share with us?
Do you know anything about sequences and series going into this course? If so, what experience do you have with them?

Part II: Take a few minutes to research applications of Infinite Sequences and/or Infinite Series. Share the application that you find the most interesting with the class and why. 

Your main post is due on Wednesday of Week 1. 

Response Post (In approximately 100 words): 

You will also read other student threads and reply to one of your classmates. Please provide a thoughtful response relevant to the post that you are replying to. For example, replying only with "Nice to meet you, X. Thanks for sharing!" could be posted on any thread rather than the specific thread you are replying to. Below are just some ideas of how to reply to your classmate:

Do you have any commonalities or share any hobbies with the poster? 
How was your application to sequences/series similar or different from the poster?
Do you have any recommendations or feedback to help the poster throughout the class?

Your response post is due on Sunday of Week 1.
