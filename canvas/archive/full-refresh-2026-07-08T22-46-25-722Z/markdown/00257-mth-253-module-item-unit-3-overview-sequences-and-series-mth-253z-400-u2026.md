Unit 3 - Overview

Introduction

In our last unit, we determined when a sequence converges by applying the infinite limit. However, simply applying the limit to a series no longer tells us if the series is convergent. It can tell us about divergence though! We learned how to determine when a Geometric Series and Telescoping Series converge, but what about series that don't fit into these two categories? How do we determine if these series converge or diverge? In this unit, we are going to learn about many different tests to determine if a series is convergent or divergent. 

Unit Learning Outcomes

After successful completion of this week, you will be able to:

Assess what conclusions can be made from applying the Divergence Test, as well as recognize when the Divergence Test fails.
Apply the basic comparison test, limit comparison test, integral test, alternating series test, ratio test, and root test to determine whether a particular series converges or diverges.
Determine if a series has absolute convergence, conditional convergence, or neither.
