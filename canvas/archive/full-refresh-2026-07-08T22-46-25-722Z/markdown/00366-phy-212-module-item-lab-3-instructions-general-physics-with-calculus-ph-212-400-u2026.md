Lab 3 Instructions
Purpose

Experimental Goal: Determine whether the measured horizontal launch distances of rolling objects support the predicted dependence on rotational inertia and identify which physical mechanisms best explain any differences between experimental results and theoretical predictions.

In this lab, you will use a physical model to predict the motion of a rolling object and test it with experimental data. By comparing theory and experiment, including trends and uncertainties, you will evaluate how well the model describes the real system.
