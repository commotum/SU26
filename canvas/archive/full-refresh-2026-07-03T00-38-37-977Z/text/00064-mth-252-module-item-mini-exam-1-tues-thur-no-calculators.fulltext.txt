1.1 Riemann Sum reminder:



With Tables and Graphs --> practice left, right, midpoint sum approximations, expanding/writing out all the terms. 

Sigma notation with function f(x) --> ONLY practice the right endpoint sum approximation using sigma notation. 
Practice identifying units for the function f, the input variable x or t, and the Riemann sum approximation. 




Practice problems

1.1: 16, 25 with right sum, 32 with right sum, 34, 39, 40, 42, 43 try left, upper, lower, and midpoint sums.

Tables of data for practice: Remember that in my lecture notes we had several tables with which to practice.


1.2: 61, 63, 75, 80,  81, 83, 92, 102, 112

Take any definite integral (try 83) and express this in sigma notation with n=25 rectangles.
