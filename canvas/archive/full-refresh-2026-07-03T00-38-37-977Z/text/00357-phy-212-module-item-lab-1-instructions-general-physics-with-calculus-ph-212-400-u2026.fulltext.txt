Purpose
Activity goal: Build community, learn how this course will work, and determine whether experimental results support a scientific claim based on uncertainty ranges.
No measurement is perfectly exact — and no scientific investigation happens in isolation. In this lab, students will begin developing the collaboration skills that are essential in physics and other technical fields by working with their peers to coordinate schedules, share responsibility, and communicate effectively. If challenges arise, students are encouraged to reach out to their instructor or TA — learning how to work productively as a team is an intentional part of the course.
This activity also introduces how scientists interpret experimental data through uncertainty. Students will revisit the ideas of measurement uncertainty and statistical uncertainty, and learn how both instrument limits and natural variation affect the values we report. Understanding how uncertainty shapes the reliability and meaning of measurements is a core skill in experimental physics and will be an important focus of this lab.
