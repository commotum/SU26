Intro
As we expand our repertoire of techniques, we will need to practice a variety of problems to build our problem-solving intuition.  To achieve mastery, we want to be able to look at a random (definite or indefinite) integral, identify structural properties, and then choose an efficient technique to evaluate or solve the integral. As practice will help achieve this mastery, below are a variety of examples, readings, and practice problems to support us with this effort.Lecture Notes (totally optional): 3.1-3.2-Lecture Notes.pdf
