Finding Group Members - Optional Step
For your project, you can choose to work alone or work in a group with up to three students total in a group. Whether you work alone or in a group, your project is to create a presentation on your topic.
Review the description of the project here: 🤔 Project | Consequences & Implications of Science
Instructions
If you would like to find group members for your project, please do the following:

Post your topic to indicate your willingness to form a group. (Or browse the topics and ask to join one.)
Wait for others to reply to your post and ask to join. 
The limit is three students per group.
Once you form your group, use your discussion thread to decide how your group will communicate going forward as you create your presentation on your topic.

Grading Criteria
This discussion is not graded. It is an optional area for you to connect with others and form a group if you wish.
