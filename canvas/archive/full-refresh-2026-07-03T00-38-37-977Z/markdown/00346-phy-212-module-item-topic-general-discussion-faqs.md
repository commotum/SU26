General Discussion & FAQs

Anyone should feel free to provide answers or suggestions to various questions.

Use the  REPLY  button on this discussion page below to ask questions relating to (assignments, deadlines, technical problems, etc.). Use this forum so that all may benefit from questions and answers provided. 
*If you have questions of a personal nature, please email your TA and instructors directly.

Student Help FAQs:
Q) I can't attend live Zoom session, what should I do?
A) The recorded lecture is posted a few hours after the lecture in the "learning materials module". These lecture topics (or modules) are outlined on the syllabus doc.   
Q) What are the differences between _asy  and M#.# assignments?
A)  Participation points are awarded for answering lecture questions. Students who attend live Zoom receive a  M#.# grade. If you did not attend the Zoom, you will see a score of zero in the gradebook and should complete the asynchronous equivalent asy assignment. The zero for the Zoom assignment will be deleted when the asy assignment is graded. The asy assignment is due in 48 hours with a deduction of 1 point per day late, capped at 50%. 
Q) What is the _pre assignment?
A)  All pre-lecture quizzes are due one hour before a live lecture starts. They build prerequisite skills needed to participate during a lecture. The answers are posted at the deadline time, these assignments cannot be submitted after a deadline for credit.
Q) What is the lab component? 
A) Labs are the required group activity for all physics courses. All lab activities (Labs 0 - 6 & Capstone) must be submitted to pass this course.
