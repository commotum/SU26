Proctorio setup and troubleshooting

Setup

Proctorio is a software that allows you to take a proctored quiz or exam at home at no cost to you.

You need to use a Chrome browser and a web cam to access Proctorio. In most cases, from a Chrome browser, you will be able to open a quiz or exam in Canvas that uses Proctorio and submit it easily.

If you need additional support, here is the main Proctorio page
Links to an external site.
 for information.

If you use a Macbook there is a specific Proctorio extension required for the quiz to work. Click the "Secure Exam Proctor" link on the left navigation bar for the course. It will take you to a page that gives details on how to download the extension. For Macbooks, generally to use programs, you have to download Safari and chrome extensions. Once you downloaded the extension by clicking the "Download Proctorio extension" link listed on the "Secure Exam Proctor" page, the colors on the left navigation menu will change color and you will be able to take the quiz once you have navigated back to the assignment.

Troubleshooting

Having trouble with Proctorio? First, remember you need to use a Chrome browser.

You may need to update Chrome and clear your history, cache and cookies.

You should not need an access code, it is supplied internally. Please see instructions in the Proctorio Guide

Proctorio tips


Proctorio will not allow you to open a new browser or browser tab. If you do this, you will be ejected from the session.

Proctor does not allow the use of hot keys and will eject you if you try to use them.

Pop-up windows will automatically eject you if they appear, please disable them while using Proctorio.

If your internet connection is unstable and cuts out, you will be ejected from Proctorio. If you do not have access to a stable internet, you should use the Zoom alternatives for quizzes and the final exam.

What if Proctorio ejects you from a quiz? You are allowed to drop three in-class quizzes, and this will count as one of your dropped quizzes. You are allowed to drop one quiz and this will count as your dropped quiz. If your system is not compatible with Proctorio, you should use the Zoom alternatives for in-class quizzes, bi-weekly quizzes and the final exam.

Alternatives to Proctorio


If you do not want to use Proctorio, you can opt to take a quiz or the final exam Proctored in the Zoom class at the normal class time or at 6pm. You can also arrange to test in an approved testing center. For that option, please contact the Ecampus Testing Center to arrange it. It may take up to two weeks for your testing location to be approved by Ecampus.

Questions in the quizzes and Final exam will be of the same form and same level of difficulty, regardless of modality. The Canvas quizzes and final exam questions in Proctorio will be chosen randomly from a bank of questions.
