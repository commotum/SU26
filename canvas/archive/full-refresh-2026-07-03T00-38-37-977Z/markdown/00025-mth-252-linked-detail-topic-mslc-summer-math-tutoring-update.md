MSLC Summer Math Tutoring Update

From the MSLC: 

Hi All, 
The MSLC opens today. Summer hours will be 10AM-4PM Monday to Thursday from today until August 14. Exam proctoring will be available whenever the MSLC is open. 
We will also hold virtual MSLC hours for students unable to attend in-person tutoring. This will be available 5PM-8PM PDT Monday-Thursday. 
More information about the MSLC, along with a link to the virtual MSLC on Teams, is available here:https://math.oregonstate.edu/undergraduate/mathematics-statistics-learning-center
