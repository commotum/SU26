Intro
Calculus is a powerful tool, especially when we learn to evaluate and generalize the notion of exact area. With the practice below, we will move from Riemann sums (discrete perspective) to integration (continuous perspective) by way of the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus (FTC). We analyze and apply both parts of the FTC in the various learning material below.  To fully leverage the power of the FTC, we will explore antiderivatives further as we go. 
**Important** Authors sometimes swap the order of FTC 1 and FTC2. Several videos below use an order that is reversed from our textbook. I will always referenced the FTC1 as the accumulation function and the FTC2 as the evaluation to match OpenStax textbook.

Click, hold, and drag the x-point on the x-axis as desired.
