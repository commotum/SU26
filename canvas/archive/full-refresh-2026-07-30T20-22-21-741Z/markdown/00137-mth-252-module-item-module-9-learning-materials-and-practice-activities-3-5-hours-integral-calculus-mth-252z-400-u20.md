Module 9 — Learning Materials and Practice Activities 🕓 3 - 5 hours

Intro

We begin the last of quarter units for the course which covers various real-world application problems. Like the graphical applications, subtle details in the setup of the problem are critical to observe.  For the physical applications in 2.5, students maximize their success by sketching out the phenomena, label the diagram, understand knowns and constants, etc. For example, for a pumping problem, draw out the full tank and consider how a single cross-section is being moved/pumped out.  Then build this up to an integral.  As we work on real-world application problems, these will require careful attention to details and units, whenever units are given. The material below will help you get started on your mastery.
