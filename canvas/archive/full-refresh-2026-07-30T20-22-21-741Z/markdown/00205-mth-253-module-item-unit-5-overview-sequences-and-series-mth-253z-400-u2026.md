Unit 5 - Overview

Introduction

We are in the final week of the course! In this week, we will learn about applications of Taylor and Maclaurin series, as well as approximating the value of a series within a particular error. For example, in the image above, what steps would we take to integrate this function? We cannot use any of the techniques learned in Integral Calculus. This is where Taylor and Maclaurin series will be used. We will also introduce a new kind of power series - in the complex (imaginary) number system. 

Weekly Learning Outcomes

After successful completion of this week, you will be able to:

Approximate complicated functions using Taylor and Maclaurin polynomials. 
Use partial sums of infinite series to approximate a sum and be able to estimate the error in the approximation.
Find the Radius and disk of convergence for a Complex power series. 
Prove Euler's Identity using a complex power series.
