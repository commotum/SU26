Module 3 | Overview of Gravity

 

Introduction

For this module, we will focus on gravitation where the system has sufficient spatial extent that the acceleration due to gravity cannot be considered constant.

Weekly Learning Outcomes

After successful completion of this week, you will be able to: 

determine whether or not the change in the acceleration due to gravity is negligible for the system
define gravitational force for an extended system
define and use Kepler's three laws concerning orbital motion
define and use Newton's law of gravity
determine the potential energy of a system of gravitationally coupled objects
analyze the behavior of orbiting satellites
analyze the behavior of a system of gravitating objects
synthesize infomation about a gravitationally coupled system based on an energy diagram
analyze the motion of a geostationary satellite
Task List

In order to achieve these learning outcomes, please make sure to complete the following:

Study the readings and lectures: Module 3 | Gravity | Learning Materials
Complete all of the required activities: Module 3 | Gravity | Required Activities
Complete the online homework
Complete all pre-lecture and participation assignments
Complete all lab assignments
Complete all quiz assignments
