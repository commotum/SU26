
            

  



    Module 5 | Overview of Waves
  
    

 
Introduction
For this module, we will focus on waves including traveling waves, standing waves, superposition and harmonics.
Weekly Learning Outcomes
After successful completion of this week, you will be able to: 

differentiate between an oscillation and a wave
define wave properties including wavelength, wave number, period, frequency, amplitude and phase
analyze and solve problems regarding one-dimensional waves
analyze and solve problems regarding two-dimensional waves
apply wave concepts to sound waves and light waves
determine the power transferred by a wave
calculate intensity and intensity levels from a wave-emitting source
evaluate intensity level in decibels
define Doppler shift
determine the Doppler shifted frequency or wavelength of a wave with a moving source
determine the Doppler shifted frequency or wavelength of a wave with a moving observer
describe the principle of superposition
determine the properties of a standing wave
formulate the spectrum of harmonics for a wave on a string
formulate the spectrum of harmonics for a wave in a pipe with open or closed ends
analyze a system exhibiting wave harmonics
analyze a system with interference
explain the concept of beating
determine the beat frequency of a superposition of waves
