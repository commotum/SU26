                Your Answer:
                Today was mainly about nonuniform circular motion. An object moving in a circle can have radial acceleration toward the center and tangential acceleration along the path. Radial acceleration changes the direction of velocity, while tangential acceleration changes speed. Net force points the same way as total acceleration, so drawing a good free-body diagram with radial and tangential axes is the key step.
