Question 2
Reflecting on collaboration:
Describe one example of good collaboration in a group setting. This could be something you’ve experienced, something you’d like to see, or something you think all lab groups should aim for.
Your Answer:

One example of good collaboration is when group members divide the work fairly, communicate about their progress, and help each other understand the lab instead of only focusing on finishing quickly. A good lab group should make sure everyone has a chance to contribute and that the final work represents shared understanding.

 
Question 3
Reflecting on accountability:
What does accountability look like in a lab group? Give an example of how someone can show accountability, or describe a situation where accountability made a difference.
Your Answer:

Accountability in a lab group means following through on the tasks you agree to complete and letting the group know early if something changes. For example, if someone is responsible for entering data or working on part of the report, they should complete it by the agreed time or communicate ahead of time if they need help.

 
Question 4
Reflecting on communication:
What’s one communication habit that’s important in a group setting, especially in an online course? You might think about texting, emailing, giving feedback, or responding to messages.
Your Answer:

One important communication habit is responding to group messages in a reasonable amount of time, especially in an online course. Even a short reply helps the group know that everyone is aware of the plan and prevents confusion about meetings, deadlines, or assigned tasks.

 
Question 5
Reflecting on respect:
How can lab partners show respect to each other during collaboration? Share a specific behavior or mindset that helps create a respectful group environment.
Your Answer:

Lab partners can show respect by listening to each other’s ideas, being patient when someone asks questions, and giving feedback in a constructive way. A respectful group environment is one where people feel comfortable contributing without worrying that their ideas will be dismissed.
