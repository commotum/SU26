Module 4 | Overview of Oscillations

 

Introduction

For this module, we will focus on simple harmonic oscillations.

Weekly Learning Outcomes

After successful completion of this week, you will be able to: 

define simple harmonic motion
define oscillations
define properties of oscillations including period, frequency, amplitude and phase
analyze systems exhibiting simple harmonic oscillations in one dimension
analyze systems exhibiting simple harmonic oscillations involving circular motion
solve and analyze kinematic motion problems involving simple harmonic motion
construct energy diagrams for systems exhibiting simple harmonic motion
solve and analyze conservation of energy problems involving simple harmonic motion
differentiate between a simple pendulum and a physical pendulum
analyze the behavior of a simple pendulum and a physical pendulum
analyze the behavior of a damped oscillator
explain the concept of resonance
explain the concept of driven oscillation
