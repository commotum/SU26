Due Jul 4 by 5pm Points 5 Submitting an external tool Available Jun 28 at 5pm - Aug 14 at 11am

Notes are required for each quiz. If you do not have a half page to a full page of notes with your photo ID in the uploaded pdf, you will not get credit for the quiz.

Please use a 8.5"x11" sheet of paper to create your note sheet. The notes need to be in your own handwriting and contain material current to the quiz.

Place your photo ID on the note sheet and take a picture of it. Convert your photo to a scan and produce a pdf to upload to Gradescope. If you are not sure how to do this, see the page on Gradescope in the Course information module.

Please follow the Gradescope link for this assignment to submit your notes and acknowledge the integrity statement for this quiz.

There is a deduction of 1 point per day for late submission, not to go below 1/2 point.
