Introduction
In the last unit, we learned about the many different tests to determine the convergence or divergence of a series. In all of those series, the terms were numerical values. In this unit, we are going to bring in variables to the series - called Power Series. Depending on the value of x, the series may converge or diverge. We will learn to find the values of x that make the series converge. Additionally, we will learn to express functions as power series, which in many cases requires the use of a Taylor Series or a Maclaurin Series.
Unit Learning Outcomes
After successful completion of this week, you will be able to: 

Determine the radius and interval of convergence for a power series.
Represent familiar functions as power series.
Perform integration and differentiation of power series. 
Convert functions into Taylor or Maclaurin polynomials.
