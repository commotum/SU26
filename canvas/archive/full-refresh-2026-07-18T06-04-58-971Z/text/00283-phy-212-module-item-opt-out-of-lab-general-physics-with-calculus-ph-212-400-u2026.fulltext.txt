
            

  



    Opt-out of lab
  
    If you are enrolled this term in PH 212 but you have completed the entire course previously here at OSU within the last two years, with a grade of D- or better, you may be eligible to opt out of (i.e. not need to attend or do) the lab portion this time.
If you opt out of lab, your grade will be based on the rest of the points possible - excluding the 100 points for lab.
Do stay enrolled in your lab section - you just don't need to attend.
If you think you may qualify, please do the following--and please do it ASAP:
-- Address a new email message to kathryn.hadley@oregonstate.edu
-- Use this subject line for the email:  
“LAB OPT-OUT REQUEST for PH 212”
-- In the body of the email, please list the following:
your name
your OSU ID
the year and term when you previously completed PH 212 here at OSU
your instructor for PH 212
the grade you earned for that term. 
After we receive such a request from you, we will verify your records and then (if all is OK) send you a confirming email to officially excuse you from the lab.  But please note:  You are not excused from lab until you receive such a confirming e-mail.
Please do everything above ASAP if you wish to opt out of lab.
