                Today’s topic was about rotational motion and how it relates to regular straight-line motion. Instead of position, velocity, and acceleration, rotational motion uses angle, angular velocity, and angular acceleration. Angular velocity describes how fast an object rotates, while angular acceleration describes how fast that angular velocity changes. We also learned that when angular velocity changes with time, we may need calculus, such as derivatives and integrals, to find quantities like angular displacement.
