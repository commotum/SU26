Using your own words, enter at least 50 words describing the concepts underlying the topic for today. You need to completely answer this question to get credit for this assignment.
Your Answer:

Circular motion involves velocity changing direction even when speed stays the same. This means an object moving in a circle is still accelerating. In uniform circular motion, that acceleration points toward the center and is called radial or centripetal acceleration. Its size depends on the object's speed and the radius of the path: higher speed increases radial acceleration, while a larger radius decreases it.
