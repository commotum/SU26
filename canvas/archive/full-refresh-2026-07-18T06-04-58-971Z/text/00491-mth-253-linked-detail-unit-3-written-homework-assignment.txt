Purpose

In this course, you will have both written homework and online homework assignments. Online homework assignments are designed to provide instant feedback and check student understanding. These assignments will only grade the final answer submitted for each question. However, the written solution, process for arriving at the final answer, and interpretation is just as important as the final answer. Additionally, this is an assignment where your instructor will provide more detailed feedback about each of your steps, rather than only concluding a correct versus incorrect final answer. 

Instructions

In this assignment, there will be five questions that you will complete relevant to the Unit 3 material. In each of these questions, please show all of your steps in clear and organized detail. It is important that you organize your work in a way so that your instructor can follow each step. Do not assume that your instructor will ‘know what you mean’ from one step to the next. The idea with these written questions is to help develop concise mathematical communication skills with current and future colleagues. 

Submission Details

For each of the five questions, you will write out your steps on paper or on a tablet. Once you have completed each question, you will upload images of your solutions into GradeScope by clicking on Written Homework #3 in Canvas. A PDF of the homework is here: Unit #3 Written Homework.pdf
