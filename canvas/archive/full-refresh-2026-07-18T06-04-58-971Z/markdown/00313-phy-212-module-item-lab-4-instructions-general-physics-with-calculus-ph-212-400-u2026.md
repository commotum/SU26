Lab 4 Instructions
Purpose

Experimental Goal: Determine the period T of a pendulum with a set length. Then investigate how the period of a pendulum depends on its length and compare to a given hypothesis.

In this lab activity, you will create a simple experiment to investigate periodic motion and practice data analysis techniques to compare your data to a given hypothesis.

 

Step 1: Individual Discussion Post Due Wednesday

Open the lab handout, then post a picture of your apparatus AND post your answers to Questions Q1–Q6 in your group's discussion thread. 

Open discussion assignment here: Lab 4 Discussion
Link to lab handout page: Lab 4 Data Analysis

 

Step 2: Meet with your Group and TA

After completing your individual work, meet with your group during your scheduled lab time to discuss your responses and address any remaining questions.

Then, check in with your TA during your assigned meeting slot. This meeting is an opportunity to ask questions, clarify instructions, and get support while completing the experiment. Attendance at the TA meeting counts toward your lab report grade for the week, email your TA if you cannot make your assigned meeting time.

 

Step 3: Submit Group Lab Report to Gradescope by Saturday

Work together to compile your group’s lab report. When it’s ready, submit it via Gradescope.

Submit the group report: Lab 4 Group Report

Important: 

➡️ Make sure all group members’ names are included on the report.

➡️ When submitting to Gradescope, link each group member’s email to the file.
