Quiz 2 Notes 
Due Saturday by 5pm Points 5 Submitting an external tool Available Jul 12 at 5pm - Aug 14 at 10am

Please go to Gradescope and submit your notes for Quiz 2 and acknowledge the integrity statement.

Notes are required for each quiz.If you do not have a half page to a full page of notes with your photo ID in the scan, you will not get credit for the quiz.

The notes need to be in your own handwriting and contain material current to the quiz.

Please use a regular sheet of paper 8.5" x 11". You are required to have at least half a page of notes and no more than one side of the page.

Place your photo ID on the note sheet and take a picture of it. Change your photo to a scan and make it into a pdf to upload to Gradescope. If you are not sure how to do this, see the page on Gradescope in the Course information module.
