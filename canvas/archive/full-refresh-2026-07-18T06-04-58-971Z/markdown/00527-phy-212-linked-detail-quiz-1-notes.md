Due Jul 4 by 5pm Points 5 Submitting an external tool Available Jun 28 at 5pm - Aug 14 at 11am

Notes are required for each quiz. If you do not have a half page to a full page of notes with your photo ID in the uploaded pdf, you will not get credit for the quiz.

Please follow the Gradescope link for this assignment to submit your notes and acknowledge the integrity statement for this quiz.
