Intro

Graphical application problems require careful attention to the details given in the hypothesis and a solid understanding of graphs. As we continue studying volumes and areas, practicing a variety of problems is optimal for building and strengthening your intuition about these various problem types. Below is content material and problems that will help you achieve your goals.
