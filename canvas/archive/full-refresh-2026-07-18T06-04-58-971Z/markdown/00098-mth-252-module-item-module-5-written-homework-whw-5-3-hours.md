Purpose

The Written Homeworks (WHWs) will give you low-stakes practice on writing full solutions to problems that pertain to the course material.  Attempting to solve these problems, whether you get a correct answer or not, is an essential part of becoming prepared for your upper-division course work.  These assignments are graded for correctness/accuracy and once in a while for completion.  

Instructions

Each Written Homework (WHW) will consist of a list of problems, either from the course textbook or an attached pdf.  These will be posted in Canvas and solutions are to be submitted in Gradescope to the corresponding assignment.

Submissions can be hand written and scanned, completed on a tablet, or typeset.
