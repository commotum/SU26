Module 3 — Overview

Introduction

This week we study the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus (FTC) and apply this to study the Net Change Theorem.  Wanting to apply the FTC to as many applications as possible, we will also investigate general rules for “undoing” derivatives learned in prior classes.  This is the study of antiderivatives.

Module Learning Outcomes

After successful completion of this module, you will be able to:

Apply the fundamental theorem of calculus to evaluate definite integrals and to differentiate integrals with respect to a limit of integration.
Summarize connections between differentiation and integration.
Solve applications involving antiderivatives.
Apply the net-change theorem.

This module aligns with the following course learning outcomes: 2-4.
