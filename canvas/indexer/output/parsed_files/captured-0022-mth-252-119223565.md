Achieve Registration Process
Your least expensive option is to purchase Achieve access through OSU Beaver Store for
$39.75. Follow these steps to register your Achieve materials.
Step I: Purchase Achieve Through OSU Beaver Store
Use the link provided by your instructor to purchase access.
Once done, you will get an email (or message) that looks like:
Title: [TITLE]
Author: [AUTHOR]
ISBN: [ISBN]
Thank you for your purchase. Your access code is: [CODE]
You now need to redeem your access code, as follows.
Step II: Pick Your Browser
Achieve works best with Chrome, and continues to be incompatible with Safari.
Another good option is Microsoft Edge.
Step III: Go to Your Canvas Course
(1) Log in to your Canvas course.
(2) Select your course for this term.
(3) Inside your Canvas course, locate and click on any Achieve assignment inside
Canvas.
• Students do NOT need a course ID to gain access.
• If you are not sure which assignments are Achieve assignments, you may instead
click Macmillan Learning in the left navigation panel of your Canvas course and
click the Achieve link.
(4) When prompted, enter your name and email address.
• If you have an existing Achieve account that you would like to link to your Canvas
account, enter the e-mail address of that Achieve account here.
(5) Enter the access code purchased in your bookstore [CODE], then click the Enter button.
• Do not register for your Achieve materials through the Achieve website. This will
prevent your Achieve grades from appearing in the Canvas gradebook.
• If you are not ready to make the purchase, when asked for the access code, you may
instead click Start a grace period and get yourself 14 days of grace period.
After 14 days, you will be required to pay for Achieve access.
