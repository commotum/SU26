Lab
Email the instructor for lab opt-outs if you previously have taken the course and passed
the lab component: see Course Information module for details
PH 212 labs are completed individually at home using accessible materials. At the end
of each week, students work with their lab group to compare data, discuss results, and
submit a group report.
To be assigned to a lab group and matched with a TA, students should complete the
following assignments this week (recommended order):
Course Introduction Discussion Post
Lab Values Survey
Lab 1 Discussion Post
Lab 1 Report (Individual)
In the Course Introduction Discussion Post, students should introduce themselves and
clearly state the days and times they are generally available to work on lab experiments
and group reports. Students from previous terms consistently report that having group
members with similar schedules makes collaboration much easier. Encourage
students to provide detailed availability and use the CTRL+F search feature to find
classmates with similar schedules.
Form a lab group in the “People” tab as explicitly outlined in the “course Introductions”
page. Once students have joined a group, they should complete the group's shared
Google Doc with their weekly availability. This document will be used to identify times
for group meetings and weekly meetings with their TA.
The Lab 1 Discussion Post is due on Thursday, so students should fill out their
availability as soon as possible and be as accurate as they can. If there are scheduling
conflicts, it is important to identify them early. We use the availability documents to
move students between groups when necessary, so completing the document is an
important part of the group formation process.
If any issues arise with group formation or scheduling, please let the Lead TA know as
soon as possible.
