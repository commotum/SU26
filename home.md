# Summer Term Home

Use this as the daily feed. Newest items go near the top, with links out to the working surfaces that hold the details.

## Page Info

- Title: Summer Term Home
- Description: Daily feed and navigation hub for summer term tasks, email triage, and course work.

## Feed

Template:

```md
### YYYY-MM-DD - Short Title

Description: One sentence explaining what changed, what needs attention, or why this was added.
Links: [Todo](tasks/todo.md), [Email Inbox](emails/emails.md), [Task Log](tasks/task_log.csv), [Email Triage CSV](emails/email_triage.csv)
```

## Active Surfaces

- [Todo](tasks/todo.md) - active academic and life tasks, organized by urgency.
- [Email Inbox](emails/emails.md) - email triage notes and message-level decisions.
